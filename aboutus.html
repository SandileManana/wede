Welcome to Secunda Mighty Stars Football Club

About Our History

Secunda Mighty Stars FC was founded in 1990 by a group of local football enthusiasts determined to give the community a club to call its own. From humble beginnings on a dusty local pitch, the club has grown into one of the region's most respected amateur sides.

  1. 1990: established in eMbalenhle
  2. 2010: first LFA champions
  3. 2016: promotion to the hollywood bets
  4. 2023: Nedbankcup Last 16

Mission Statement

To develop local talent, compete with pride, and give the Secunda community a team worth rallying behind.

  • Community

    Rooted in Secunda and eMbalenhle, built by and for the people who support us.

  • Development

    Investing in youth talent and giving local players the opportunity to rise and climb the ranks of South African football.

  • Passion

    Playing with heart, on and off the pitch.

Our Home Ground

Secunda Stadium has been home to Secunda Stars since 2020. The stadium was built to help locals thrive in sports.

In the Community

Beyond match days, Secunda Mighty Stars runs free weekend coaching clinics for local youth and partners with area schools to promote sport and education together.
